- Background: 

I have been paying for a lot of different platform to manage my tasks: Monday.com, Clickup.com, Jira, Atlatisan. 

All of them are very good in their own regards, but I want a strip down version tailor to my needs - and also I don’t want to pay that much per seat anymore, this project started out like that. 

The consensus is that, all of the features are bloated inside these platform and so many function and features that I don’t even use, I want something simpler

The thinking process was similar to how Tesla and Apple made their UI/UX so intuitive, you don’t have to think or stop and think:


“Oh maybe I should change this” “Maybe I should add an automation here” “Oh the tasks are not there yet, let me make a group, oh wait there’s no grouping and subtasks are not there, what the hell is child issue even mean???” “What about waterfall or agile workflow is?”

With that in mind, this is why I want the platform to be intuitive, I want it to be a breeze, not tailored to anyone, but me. And how I work.

- My workflow and short bio: 

Bio: I’m an Executive Producer, 10+ years in film and commercial Production spanning from EMEA and APAC, I’m working most of the time independently.

Context: I’m constantly checking my email, approve with new edit/design/strategy/business inquiry/ operation/ sourcing/ etc… I need to look into the project and see how every project is running, in an intuitive way, where is the bottleneck, where could be improve, and so much more questions that is related to operation. And on top of that I need to manage my personal life, so I want to hands off as much as I can so I can focus creatively how the business can grow and not needing to micro manage.


Workflow: currently I’m building a Youtube channel with a founder of a company, the channel is called: “The Meals That Matter”



The mission we set out to do: 

After working in Production for a very long time, we are tired of working for other people, and there is a niche that we found could might be interesting to everyone globally.

As the world it seems like a lot of spectacle in all aspect of life, we want to tell the normal daily stories of normal human being, it could be your mom 



The current team we have for production (per production):

Director

Story Producer

Producer (Field & Line & Sourcing)

DP

Cam OP (1 person)

AC (2 person) - also act as grip and gaffer.

Optional: Driver


The current workflow we have:

Producer to source out talent

Producer to have the first discovery call

Producer to write down their production brief about their story and to filter out stories once we have a stories bank

After confirmation, Story producer to come in and have another call with the talent that has been selected, discovering deeper into their story. Thus we come to this point whether if the talent is good enough for us to start with production

After the Story Producer call, they will then start with the Story Outline, then the Overall Outline, afterward Interview questions.

Once that has been establish, the producer will also work in parallel, figuring out from the story outline or directly talking with the Story Producer to point out what type of food they are making, then proceed to ask the talent via phone call or any means of communication to work out the details.

On the producer side of things: the producer once figure out the food, they need to dive deeper into breaking down the scene, currently we have around 4 scene in total: Interview scene, Cooking scene, Hero/B-roll in general to establish the location or environment they are in, dedicate character establishing scene. Afterward the Producer has to figure out the logistic.

During that time, the producer has to keep in mind about the budget: budget constraint, logistic for going to location, sourcing the right vendor, allocating time and breaking down the scene for all department to work effectively.

Criteria we have for the shoot: We shoot 2 location per day, which also means we have 2 episodes - 2 talents needed to be filmed, we want to be making the standard how you can do Cinematic level of visual Documentary film in Youtubesque format, thus why we want a effective workflow to work and can be scale effectively

Going back to the production: next up is with the DP, our crew runs lean for the whole production cycle, just to keep it nimble and less clutter with setting up too many lights, we try to use or mimic normal sun light to preserve the natural environment as much as possible.

After the documentation from Story Producer and Logistic notes has been made by the Producer (with Talent recce photos & videos) - the DP will start to work on their own version of shot-list, also given some reference from Story Producer how they want the overall mood of the story to feel.

After DP has done with shot list, we have PPM, then once the whole team align on everything, EP or Producer will also confirmed maybe that day or preferably days before that when to start Principle Photography (accounted in travel time)

During Production: EP/Producer should be managing the shoot to go on schedule, all the other department has to protect the story and enough footage as much as possible.

After production, in order of apperance: Proxies, DP notes on tech specs or otg notes, Producer notes, Story Producer to transfer editor’s brief, Director treatment (if possible). Will be hand over to Editor

Post Production: Editor to edit in less than 3 days per episodes: From assembly to final edit, then pass through or maybe for later to have a LUT dedicated from Colorist.

Social Media Team: Utilizing all the information and from certain point in the editing has to get a rough estimate and clear content calendar what needs to be publish, at what time and clear scope how much and how many content pieces should be published.

Data cleaning & gathering: constant update from Social Media team to report and analyze, giving clear instruction or report to the whole company what works and what is going to be trending - if applicable to the team.



Pain point:



This seems not much, but without a dedicated PM, the workflow and checking up with every steps in other project management platform becomes a very tedious tasks, I want to automate this process to streamline and less managing or manual labor work.

Focusing more on Sourcing talents - real people, real story, impactful meaning to the world.

Focusing more on optimizing the workflow - everyone comes home happy with their product they have made. Not running down the whole team.

So that’s a short intro about the reasoning why I am building this platform, from this structure of work, tell me what you understand about me, how can I utilize my project in building this website/ application to be better.
